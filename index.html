About Trigger

A national stage for student talent

We, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, RajaRajeswari College of Engineering, Bengaluru, proudly organise TRIGGER, a national level technical symposium, and have done so for the past three years with great success. We host a range of technical and non-technical events where students can showcase their talents, and every year participants from colleges across India encourage us to keep it going.

Like previous years, this year we host TRIGGER 2K18 on 26th October 2018 with the theme 'Ignite Yourself' — a platform for students across the nation to exhibit their innovations.

— Team Trigger
